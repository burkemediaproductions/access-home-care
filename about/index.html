Meet the Team

Professional, warm, and consistent care — backed by clear communication with families.

Preston Miller, RN

President & CEO

More than two decades ago, Preston faced a challenge many families know too well: the struggle to find dependable, high-quality care for a loved one. That personal experience, paired with his lifelong respect and compassion for seniors, sparked a mission to make professional, reliable home care easier to access for families throughout the Central Valley.

With nearly three decades of experience in the home care industry, Preston’s expertise is backed by a deep clinical foundation. He earned his Registered Nurse (RN) licensure in 1992 and a Bachelor’s degree in Healthcare Management, eventually serving in the ICU and cardiac care units at Doctors Medical Center before transitioning his focus entirely to home care.

When Access Senior Home Care launched in 2011, it was more than a business, it was a commitment to the community. As a Central Valley local, Preston genuinely enjoys being a trusted resource to seniors and their loved ones, whether they are Access clients or simply seeking guidance. His focus remains the same today as it was at the beginning: helping people stay safe, supported, and comfortable at home with care that is both compassionate and expertly managed.

Paige Miller

General Manager

Paige joined the Access team in 2013 as a dedicated caregiver, gaining invaluable firsthand experience in senior care. Her talent for detail and organization led her to become Billing Manager in 2014, before she transitioned into her current role as General Manager in 2015. By directing our recruitment and internal teams, she ensures Access maintains the highest standards of care and professionalism. Today, she keeps operations running smoothly by overseeing accurate billing, coordinating long-term care insurance claims, and ensuring ongoing client satisfaction. With over a decade of experience, she ensures every family receives the same level of dedicated support she provided on her very first day.

Teresa Bryan-Miller

Account Executive & Quality Care Liaison

Teresa joined Access in 2024 as our Account Executive, bringing a Bachelor of Arts in Sociology and over three decades of dedicated experience in the geriatric and home health industry. In her dual role, she develops key marketing strategies, evaluates the unique needs of you or your loved one, and conducts start of care introductions to ensure a seamless transition into our services. To ensure the highest standards of care, Teresa performs regularly scheduled home visits to monitor ongoing client and family satisfaction and stays current on all client matters. By staying closely involved in every case, she serves as a knowledgeable advocate who ensures our care remains consistent, personal, and well managed.
